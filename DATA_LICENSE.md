# Data licence

The labels and predictions made by the backbar-vision authors are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Credit: "The backbar-vision authors".

This covers:

- Labels: `data/labelled/`, the `labels.csv`, `boxes.csv` and OCR files under `data/crops/` and `data/crops_whole/`,
  `data/test_bars/manifest.csv` and `phashes.txt`, and the judgements in `data/test_bars/`.
- Predictions and results: `data/predictions*.csv` and `data/results.csv`.

Not covered:

- The Wikimedia Commons photos in `data/test_bars/img/`. Each keeps its own licence, listed in `data/test_bars/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
- Bar photos found through web search without a known licence. They are not in this repository; their sources are in
  `data/test_bars/manifest.csv` and all rights stay with their owners.
- Brand names, logos and label designs visible on the bottles, which belong to their owners.
- Source code, which is under the Apache License 2.0 (see `LICENSE`).

None of our own photos (the kitchen and pour photos, and crops of them) are published.
